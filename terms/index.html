5. Apple Watch and Apple Health

Stantia may offer an optional Apple Watch companion experience and Apple Health (HealthKit) integration for Train session metrics. Watch measuring is started from iPhone Train; the Watch companion shows live metrics and an end-of-session summary when measuring is active.

You choose whether to save the session.
