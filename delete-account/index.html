Delete your account

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Stantia account and what happens to your data. Stantia is a social training app for hybrid athletes.

1. Delete from the Stantia app

The fastest way to delete your account is inside the app:

  1. Open Stantia and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm the deletion.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will be signed out once the request completes.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email us to request account deletion:

fmatamorosa09@icloud.com

Please include:

  • The subject line: Stantia account deletion request
  • The email address associated with your Stantia account
  • Your username (if you remember it)
  • A short confirmation that you want the account permanently deleted

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before completing the request. We aim to process email deletion requests within 30 days.

3. What data is deleted

When your Stantia account is deleted, we remove or permanently de-identify personal data tied to your account that we are not legally required to keep, including:

  • Account credentials and athlete profile (email, username, display name, bio, avatar, specialties, preferences)
  • Posts, comments, and media you created
  • Workouts and workout collections you created
  • Train session history and ratings you submitted
  • Social graph data (follows, likes, saves, blocks) associated with your account
  • In-app notifications and reports you submitted

4. What may be retained

After deletion, we may retain limited information for a reasonable period when needed for:

  • Security, fraud prevention, or abuse investigation
  • Dispute resolution
  • Legal, tax, or regulatory obligations
  • Backup systems that are overwritten on a normal rotation schedule

Content that other athletes created about you (for example a mention in their post) may remain if it is not part of your own account data. Aggregated or anonymized analytics that no longer identify you may also remain.

5. Related policies

For more detail on how Stantia handles personal information, see the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
