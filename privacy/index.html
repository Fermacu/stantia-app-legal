Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stantia (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Stantia mobile application and related services.

1. Who we are

Stantia is a social training app for hybrid athletes. Training is the content; community is the product. You can create an account, publish workouts and posts, follow other athletes, train in private crews, train in-app, and discover content by specialty.

Contact: soporte@stantia.app

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Stantia, we may collect:

  • Account information — email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider), username, and display name. New accounts are also asked for country, city or province, date of birth, and gender (female, male, or other, including if you do not want to specify). You choose whether your country flag is shown next to your name. Bio, avatar, specialties, and feed preferences stay optional. Accounts created before these details were collected can keep using Stantia without them, and can add them later in Edit profile.
  • User-generated content — workouts, posts, comments, session history, ratings, saved items, and other content you create or upload (including profile photos, workout covers, and post photos).
  • Garmin Connect activity data (optional) — if you connect Garmin in Stantia Settings, Garmin may send us activity summaries after you train on a Garmin device and sync with Garmin Connect (for example sport type, start time, duration, distance, pace or speed, heart rate, and calories). We store that activity on your Stantia account as a History session. Details are in Garmin Connect below.
  • Health and fitness metrics (optional, Apple platforms / iOS) — when you use Train with a paired Apple Watch and grant Health access, we may process workout-related metrics such as heart rate (including average, maximum, and time in heart-rate zones), active energy (calories), distance, pace or speed, elevation ascended, steps, cadence, power, swimming stroke count and pool lengths (including related swim stats such as SWOLF when available), and per-kilometer splits on run or walk sessions. These metrics may be shown live on iPhone and Apple Watch, stored with your completed Train session summary on device, optionally uploaded to Stantia’s servers when cloud upload is enabled, and written to Apple Health on your devices when you allow it. Android and iPhone without a Watch do not collect HealthKit live metrics; Train still works without this data.
  • Insights and public profile metrics — we compute Hybrid Index, specialty split, activity trends, and related training insights from your completed sessions. By default, Hybrid Index and specialty split on your public Stats tab are private. If you turn on Public profile metrics in Settings, other athletes can see those insights on your profile, and you may be included in monthly athlete rankings that require public metrics.
  • Shared session images — when you share a session card or Stories sticker, the image is composed on your device from the session fields and modules you choose to include (for example distance, time, pace, heart rate, calories, splits, zones, route drawing, or notes). Public feed posts do not automatically attach your GPS route. What appears on a sticker you export is under your control for that share.
  • App preferences — settings such as appearance (light/dark and accent), UI language (English / Español), push notification preference, Watch metrics cloud upload (iOS), and public profile metrics stay with your account or on device as needed to apply them. Language preference can also set the language of push notification copy when push is enabled.
  • Subscription and entitlement data (Stantia Pro) — if you use or manage Stantia Pro, we store subscription status, plan or product identifiers, entitlement flags (for example features unlocked for Pro), and related billing lifecycle events needed to provide paid features. When real App Store or Google Play billing is enabled, purchase validation may involve Apple or Google; we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Route and location data from Apple Watch GPS (optional) — during outdoor locomotion sessions (such as runs, rides, hikes, rows, and kayaks) that the Apple Watch is measuring, the Watch may use its GPS to record a route. We process the route points (a polyline) only to draw the path of your session, on the complete screen and on your History record. Your iPhone does not record GPS for session metrics; distance, pace, and routes come from the Apple Watch. The route stays on your device until the session ends, then one compact route is saved with the session. If cloud upload is off, the route stays on your device only.
  • Session details you enter — title, notes, specialties, date, duration, calories, and, when relevant, distance and pace. These can come from a live session, a manual log, or values you type after you finish.
  • Social graph data — who you follow, who follows you, likes, saves, reposts, blocks, reports, crew memberships, and collaboration invites you send or receive. In crews, we also record which members have opened a conversation so seen-by indicators can be shown to other members.
  • Crew content — posts, photos, and attachments you share inside a crew. That content is visible to current members of that crew, not on the Home feed.
  • Announcements and notices — Stantia may send custom announcements (as a push, when push is on) and in-app notices to selected audiences, such as everyone, a group of athletes, athletes of a specific specialty, or specific people. Audiences may be based on profile details like gender or specialties. Announcements appear in Activity, and notices you close stay under Activity → Notices.
  • Push notification token — if you allow notifications, a device token so we can deliver alerts when the app is in the background. You can turn push off in Settings. The in-app Activity inbox still works.
  • Device and usage data — basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service (for example app version, authentication session state, and crash/diagnostic signals if enabled).
  • Support communications — messages you send to soporte@stantia.app.

We do not require access to your contacts, microphone, or advertising identifiers for core Stantia features, and the core app does not need your phone’s location. Outdoor sessions measured by a paired Apple Watch may use the Watch’s location to record a route, as described above. Photo library access is requested only when you choose a profile photo, workout cover, or post photo. Apple Health / HealthKit access is requested only when you start Train metrics with a paired Apple Watch companion.

3. Apple Watch, Apple Health, and HealthKit

On iPhone, Stantia can work with an optional Apple Watch companion app and Apple’s HealthKit framework to capture Train session metrics.

  • Optional — Live Watch metrics require a paired Apple Watch with the Stantia Watch app. If you do not use a Watch, or you decline Health permission or the in-app disclosure, Train still works and we do not collect HealthKit metrics.
  • What we access — with your permission, Stantia may read and write workout-related Health data needed for Train (for example heart rate and zones, active energy, distance, pace or speed, elevation, steps, cadence, power, swim strokes and lengths, workout samples, and, for outdoor sessions, the route recorded by the Watch). We request access through an in-app disclosure (including that aggregate metrics may be uploaded to Stantia’s servers) and then the system Health permission prompts.
  • How we use it — to show live metrics during Train on iPhone and the Watch companion, include summaries and routes on completed sessions and history on your device, optionally upload aggregate session metrics and routes to Stantia’s servers when cloud upload is enabled, and save workouts to Apple Health so they can appear alongside your other activity on your devices.
  • Phone and Watch — workouts start from iPhone Train. When a Watch is detected, Stantia asks whether you want to measure with Apple Watch. If you decline, Train runs without Watch metrics or routes. After a Watch-measured session, distance, pace, and Watch-measured calories are shown on the complete screen as measured and cannot be edited there. Turning off cloud upload keeps HealthKit-derived fields and routes on this device only while local History may still show them.
  • What we do not do — we do not sell Health or fitness data, and we do not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data-broker purposes. Health metrics are not required to use the social features of Stantia.
  • Your control — you can change Health permissions anytime in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices (or the equivalent Health settings screen), turn off Upload Watch metrics to cloud in the Stantia app Settings to keep HealthKit-derived metrics and routes on this device only (turning it on lets us upload aggregate metrics and routes to Stantia’s servers), and stop using Watch metrics by declining the disclosure or ending Watch-connected Train sessions.

4. Garmin Connect

Connecting Garmin is optional. You train on your Garmin device as usual. After Garmin Connect syncs the activity, Garmin may push that data to Stantia’s servers. We associate it with the Stantia account that authorized the connection. Stantia does not control the watch during the workout and does not request historical Garmin activities on Starter access.

How we use Garmin user data

We use Garmin activity data only to:

  • Create or update a training session in your Stantia History
  • Show duration, distance, pace, heart rate, calories, and sport on that session, Insights, streaks, rankings, and Programs compliance when those features use your History
  • Let you share that session yourself (a post, Stories card, or crew), if you choose

We do not sell Garmin user data. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, or data-broker purposes. We do not use Garmin data, or anything derived from it, to train, evaluate, or improve AI models.

Third parties that receive or process Garmin user data

Besides Garmin (the source of the data, under Garmin’s terms), the following process Garmin-derived activity records on our behalf:

  • Google LLC (Firebase / Google Cloud) — Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Storage. This is our hosting and backend. Cloud Functions receive Garmin’s push notifications; Firestore stores the resulting session on your account.

We do not send Garmin user data to advertising networks, analytics vendors, AI providers, or other fitness apps. Other Stantia athletes see Garmin-derived details only if you share a session or post that includes them.

Sale or sharing

Garmin user data is not sold. It is not shared with a third party for that party’s own use, except: (1) Google Cloud/Firebase as our processor, as listed above; (2) other Stantia users, only when you publish or share a session; (3) if required by law or to protect safety, as described in this policy.

AI Transparency Statement

Stantia does not process Garmin user data with AI systems. We do not use Garmin data to train, evaluate, or improve AI models, and Garmin does not authorize that use. Because we do not run AI on Garmin data, there is no AI consent to grant or withdraw for that processing. If this ever changed, we would update this policy first, obtain explicit consent, and give you an easy way to withdraw it before any such processing.

Access, correction, deletion, export, and withdrawing consent

  • Access and correct — open the session in History in the Stantia app, or email soporte@stantia.app to request a copy or a correction.
  • Export — email soporte@stantia.app and we will provide an export of Garmin-derived sessions tied to your account where technically feasible.
  • Delete a session — delete it in History. That removes it from Stantia; it does not delete the original activity in Garmin Connect.
  • Withdraw consent / disconnect — disconnect Garmin in Stantia Settings, or revoke Stantia in Garmin Connect. We then stop receiving new activities. Existing History sessions stay until you delete them or delete your account, unless you ask us to remove them.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete account, or our Delete account page. That removes Garmin connection records and associated personal data we are not required to keep.

Garmin API credentials are issued only to Stantia and are not shared with, transferred to, or used by any third party. If someone asks us for those keys, we will not provide them.

5. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account and athlete profile
  • Provide feed, search, follow, like, save, train, crews, weekly crew rankings, collaborations, and monthly athlete rankings
  • Display public profile fields and published content to other athletes
  • Provide optional Apple Watch / HealthKit Train metrics, routes, and session summaries
  • Import optional Garmin Connect activities into your History after you connect Garmin
  • Compute Hybrid Index and activity insights, and — only when you enable public profile metrics — show those insights on your Stats tab to others and include you in eligible monthly rankings
  • Provide Stantia Pro entitlements and subscription management when you use paid features
  • Send in-app and, if you enable them, push notifications for social activity (follows, likes, comments, replies, reposts, mentions, saves, crew invites, crew messages, collaboration invites, and when someone shares or recommends your public workout), and to deliver Stantia announcements and in-app notices
  • Moderate the community (including reports and blocks)
  • Improve reliability, security, and product quality
  • Respond to support and privacy requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

6. How information is shared

  • Other Stantia users — public profile fields, public posts, and public workouts are visible to other athletes. Your country flag is visible only if you turn on “Show flag.” City or province, date of birth, and gender are stored with your account and are not shown on your public profile. Private workouts remain in your library until you choose to share them. HealthKit metrics from Train are part of your session experience; they are not posted publicly unless you choose to share a session summary, Stories sticker, or similar content yourself. Public posts do not include your GPS route. Hybrid Index and specialty split stay private on your profile unless you enable public profile metrics. When you repost someone’s post, your followers can see that repost with your name on it, even if they do not follow the original author.
  • App stores and billing partners — when you purchase or manage Stantia Pro through Apple or Google, those platforms process payment according to their terms. We receive entitlement and transaction identifiers needed to unlock features, not your full payment card number.
  • Crew members — posts you publish in a crew are visible to current members of that crew, including captions, photos, and attached sessions or workouts. Members can also see who has seen a message and who liked it.
  • Collaborators — if someone accepts your invite, their name appears on your post, the post can appear on their profile, and a session on that post is copied into their History as their own record. They can edit or delete that copy without changing yours.
  • Apple Health on your devices — when you grant permission, workout data may be written to Apple Health / HealthKit on your Apple devices and governed by Apple’s Health settings and policies in addition to this Privacy Policy.
  • Service providers — we use trusted infrastructure providers to host and operate Stantia. Today this includes Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) to store account data, content, media, and session records (which may include aggregate Train metrics you captured when cloud upload is enabled), and to run backend logic. The same infrastructure processes Garmin activity records when you have connected Garmin, as described in Garmin Connect.
  • Garmin — if you connect Garmin, Garmin processes your device activities under Garmin’s policies and sends authorized activity data to Stantia. We do not give Garmin API keys to any third party.
  • Legal and safety — we may disclose information if required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect athletes, Stantia, or others from harm, abuse, or fraud.

7. Account deletion and your choices

You can:

  • Edit profile information and feed preferences in Settings, including country, whether your flag is shown, city or province, date of birth, and gender. If your account was created before those questions existed, you can add them whenever you want. You are not required to fill them in to keep using the app.
  • Delete posts and manage content you control
  • Block other users and report content that violates guidelines
  • Manage Apple Health permissions on your device, choose whether to use Watch-connected Train metrics, and turn off Upload Watch metrics to cloud in Stantia Settings so HealthKit-derived metrics and routes stay on this device only
  • Connect or disconnect Garmin in Stantia Settings, revoke Stantia in Garmin Connect, and request access, correction, deletion, or export of Garmin-derived sessions as described in Garmin Connect
  • Keep Hybrid Index and specialty split private, or turn on Public profile metrics if you want others (and monthly rankings) to see them
  • Manage Stantia Pro from Settings when available, including canceling according to App Store or Google Play rules when those stores handle billing
  • Delete your account in-app (Settings → Delete account), or follow the steps on our Delete account page. Account deletion removes your account and associated personal data we are not legally required to retain, including profile data and content tied to your account, subject to processing time and backups. Workout samples already saved in Apple Health remain under your Apple Health controls unless you delete them there.

You may also email soporte@stantia.app to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable under local law.

8. Data retention

We retain account and content data while your account is active, including Train session summaries and any associated metrics stored with your Stantia account. After you delete your account, we remove or de-identify personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep limited records for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Data stored in Apple Health on your devices is retained according to your Apple Health settings. Garmin-derived sessions stored with your Stantia account are retained while the account is active, unless you delete the session or disconnect and ask us to remove them. Disconnecting Garmin stops new imports; it does not by itself erase History you already saved.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards provided by our cloud providers, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong unique password and keep your device secure.

10. Children’s privacy

Stantia is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact soporte@stantia.app and we will take appropriate steps.

11. International processing

Stantia may process information on servers located outside your country (including through Firebase/Google Cloud regions). By using the app, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations subject to applicable safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Stantia evolves. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, when changes are material, provide additional notice in the app or by email when appropriate. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
