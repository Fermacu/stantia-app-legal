Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HYBRD (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the HYBRD mobile application and related services.

1. Who we are

HYBRD is a social training app for hybrid athletes. Training is the content; community is the product. You can create an account, publish workouts and posts, follow other athletes, train in-app, and discover content by specialty.

Contact: fmatamorosa98@icloud.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use HYBRD, we may collect:

  • Account information — email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider), username, display name, and optional profile details such as bio, country, avatar, specialties, and feed preferences.
  • User-generated content — workouts, posts, comments, session history, ratings, saved items, and other content you create or upload (including profile and workout images).
  • Social graph data — who you follow, who follows you, likes, saves, blocks, and reports you submit.
  • Device and usage data — basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service (for example app version, authentication session state, and crash/diagnostic signals if enabled).
  • Support communications — messages you send to fmatamorosa98@icloud.com.

We do not require access to your contacts, precise location, microphone, or advertising identifiers for core HYBRD features. Photo library access is requested only when you choose to set a profile photo or workout cover.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account and athlete profile
  • Provide feed, search, follow, like, save, train, and ranking features
  • Display public profile fields and published content to other athletes
  • Send in-app notifications related to social activity
  • Moderate the community (including reports and blocks)
  • Improve reliability, security, and product quality
  • Respond to support and privacy requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How information is shared

  • Other HYBRD users — public profile fields, public posts, and public workouts are visible to other athletes. Private workouts remain in your library until you choose to share them.
  • Service providers — we use trusted infrastructure providers to host and operate HYBRD. Today this includes Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) to store account data, content, and media, and to run backend logic.
  • Legal and safety — we may disclose information if required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect athletes, HYBRD, or others from harm, abuse, or fraud.

5. Account deletion and your choices

You can:

  • Edit profile information and feed preferences in Settings
  • Delete posts and manage content you control
  • Block other users and report content that violates guidelines
  • Delete your account in-app (Settings → Delete account), or follow the steps on our Delete account page. Account deletion removes your account and associated personal data we are not legally required to retain, including profile data and content tied to your account, subject to processing time and backups.

You may also email fmatamorosa98@icloud.com to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable under local law.

6. Data retention

We retain account and content data while your account is active. After you delete your account, we remove or de-identify personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep limited records for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards provided by our cloud providers, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong unique password and keep your device secure.

8. Children’s privacy

HYBRD is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact fmatamorosa98@icloud.com and we will take appropriate steps.

9. International processing

HYBRD may process information on servers located outside your country (including through Firebase/Google Cloud regions). By using the app, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations subject to applicable safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as HYBRD evolves. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, when changes are material, provide additional notice in the app or by email when appropriate. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact
